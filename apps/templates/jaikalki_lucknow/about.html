A Journey of Innovation and Growth

Every great journey starts with a single step, and for Jaikalki Technology, that step began two years ago as a proprietorship focused on web development. In March 2023, fueled by our passion and commitment, we evolved into Jaikalki Technology Pvt. Ltd., a dynamic company offering a wide array of IT services, with operations driven by our dedicated team in Lucknow.

From our modest start as freelancers to becoming a flourishing Private Limited company, we have consistently provided innovative solutions tailored to the distinct needs of our clients. Today, Jaikalki Technology Pvt. Ltd., with operations driven by our expert team in Lucknow, takes pride in offering three specialized divisions, each focused on driving success and growth.

  • Web Tech – Redefining digital presence with cutting-edge web solutions.

  • Gravity DigiPayment – Simplifying financial transactions for businesses with seamless payment solutions.

At Jaikalki Technology Pvt Ltd, we treat our clients like family, prioritizing long-term relationships and delivering superior solutions that drive growth.
