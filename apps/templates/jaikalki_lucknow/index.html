Gravity DigiPayment

At Gravity DigiPayment, a division of Jaikalki Technology Pvt Ltd, we revolutionize the way businesses manage payments by providing seamless, secure Pay-In solutions. With operations driven by our dedicated Lucknow team, we collaborate with multiple banks and PAPG companies to help businesses acquire Merchant IDs (MIDs) effortlessly and implement fast, reliable, and scalable payment processing systems.

  • Cutting-edge, secure, and highly efficient Pay-In payment processing solutions, ensuring swift and protected transactions for businesses across various industries, supported by our Lucknow-based operations.
  • Tailored Pay-In solutions designed to adapt to the specific needs of different business sectors, offering flexibility, security, and seamless scalability in payment handling.
  • Hassle-free merchant onboarding and seamless integration, powered by our strategic partnerships with leading banks and PAPG companies, streamlining the process of obtaining MIDs and enabling quick payment acceptance.
  • Enhanced operational workflows that minimize processing time, reduce manual errors, and improve customer payment experiences through automation and optimized transaction handling.
  • Comprehensive reporting and analytics tools that empower businesses with real-time transaction tracking, performance monitoring, and data-driven insights for smarter decision-making.
  • Round-the-clock expert support and guidance from our Lucknow-based team, ensuring businesses receive continuous assistance from onboarding to transaction management and beyond.
