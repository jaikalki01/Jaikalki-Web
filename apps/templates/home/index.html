About Us

Empowering Businesses with Seamless Solutions

As a Technology Service Provider (TSP) under the Gravity DigiPayment division of Jaikalki Technology Pvt Ltd, we bridge the gap between merchants and financial institutions by connecting with multiple banks and PAPG companies to secure MID (Merchant IDs).

Our Pay-In Solution ensures seamless payment collection services, providing businesses with secure and efficient payment acceptance across multiple platforms.

We bridge the gap between businesses and their financial needs by offering seamless Pay In and Pay Out solutions through a network of trusted partners.

Our expertise extends beyond payments, empowering clients with robust web and mobile app development and innovative API integration services.

Our mission is to simplify and enhance the financial transaction process for businesses, ensuring efficient, secure, and transparent operations.

We strive to be the go-to partner for comprehensive digital solutions that drive growth and success for our clients.

We envision a future where every business, regardless of size, can easily navigate their financial landscape with confidence and agility.

By leveraging cutting-edge technology and partnerships, we aim to revolutionize the payment and app development industry for a connected world.
